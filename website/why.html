why

if you wanted an answer in one line: jdownloader2 is closed and java, this is the opposite.

jdownloader2 works. that's the problem.

jdownloader2 earned its users: the grabber that turns 10 links into a ticked-off package, the rubric-free place for your queue. it's closed and it's java. windows runs it fine. linux doesn't.

what this isn't

  • not an interoperable clone of jdownloader2 (no jd2 assets, no jd2 assets copied)
  • not a replacement for your password manager, your vpn, your torrent client
  • not "smarter" — it does exactly what you tell it, and shows you what it decided

what it keeps from the upstream

  • the engine (segmented HTTP, resume, per-host caps, checksums)
  • the Rust binary and the tray
  • the categories sidebar layout

what this fork owns

  • the link grabber done like JD2: packages by folder, by volume, by file family
  • the packagizer for the 5% of links that need name surgery
  • the JD2-look theme (flat, dense, mono-ish, green Start / red Stop)
  • lowering the barrier: one binary, fontconfig, XDG conventions, no java

what it refuses to do

  • phone home (no analytics, no account, no telemetry)
  • install an updater that silently replaces the running binary
  • be a "supercharger for your network" or "accelerate downloads"
